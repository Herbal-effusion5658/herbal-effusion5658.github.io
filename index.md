I am **Yiyang Zhao**, a research intern at the **Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory**, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and the life sciences. I received my bachelor's education at **Zhejiang University**, majoring in Biological Sciences and Ecology with a minor in Economics. I graduated with a GPA of **3.97/4.30** and ranked **first in my class for three consecutive years**.

My interests center on **AI for Science**, **computational biology**, and **synthetic biology**. I am especially interested in building multimodal biological models and data resources, and in translating computational ideas into experimentally grounded systems.

Research & Selected Competition Projects
======

### Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory - Research Intern

**SciVerse Scientific Intelligence Database · Current**

- Contribute to the development of the SciVerse scientific intelligence database, including ADMET data resources, molecular-semantic knowledge base components, and multimodal unified modeling.
- Serve as second author, following three co-first authors, on [*BioMatrix: Towards a Comprehensive Biological Foundation Model Spanning the Modality Matrix of Sequences, Structures, and Language*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22138), with contributions to experiments and manuscript preparation.

### ZJU-China 2025 - Team Captain and Project Designer

**iGEM and SynBio competitions · February 2025 - November 2025**

- Independently conceived the project and led the wet-lab, modeling, operations, and institutional coordination teams.
- Directed the development of **LUMAMANTA**, integrating a biological module, a hardware chamber, and a software control platform.
- Designed a detection strategy combining allosteric transcription factor recognition, localized DNA strand displacement, and a PSR amplification cascade.
- Earned a **Silver Medal at the 2025 iGEM Competition** and the **Grand Prize, Gold Medal, and Best Environment/Agriculture Project Award** at SynBio Challenges 2025 ([Zhejiang University report](https://zjubiolab.zju.edu.cn/page/article/show/9df4b48a3e2044bb8e42d0e603f47a1e)).
- [Visit the ZJU-China 2025 project website](https://2025.igem.wiki/zju-china/).

### TruSense 2025 - Zhejiang University Team Member

**International biosensor and biomedical innovation competitions · November 2024 - August 2025**

- Investigated enzyme-electrode compatibility by comparing enzyme ratios, electrode materials, and immobilization agents including glutaraldehyde, trehalose, and PEG.
- Evaluated immobilization efficiency and multi-enzyme stability using cyclic voltammetry and square-wave voltammetry.
- Explored tri-enzyme fusion design through artificial intelligence and molecular simulation.
- At **SensUs 2025** in Eindhoven, received the **Public Inspiration Award (1st place)**, **Innovation Award (3rd place)**, and a **Final Medal** ([Zhejiang University report](https://zjubiolab.zju.edu.cn/page/article/show/9df4b48a3e2044bb8e42d0e603f47a1e)).
- Also received a **National Bronze Award** in a national medical innovation competition (Regional First Prize) and a **Gold Award** in a synthetic biology competition.

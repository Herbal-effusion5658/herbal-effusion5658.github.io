🤔 What is beichen-pi-desktop?

beichen-pi-desktop is a local-first, minimalist AI agent platform designed for Windows. Think of it as a simple, powerful assistant that lives on your computer—not in the cloud. It works directly with local AI models you run yourself, keeping your data private and offline.

Instead of complicated setup or subscription fees, this app gives you a clean, straightforward way to talk to your own AI models. It includes an explicit context lifecycle management system, meaning you always know exactly what information the AI is using to generate its responses. This puts you in control—you can manage, clear, or adjust the context anytime.
